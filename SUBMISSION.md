# Submission

**Title:** Frog at Dusk: Nightfall Survival

**Description**

Survive one full day as a frog: daylight, sunset, then a 2-minute night. I added a day/night cycle, ponds, rocks and trees, collectible fireflies, gifts (shield, cloak, Moon Jump, Pond Leap), four enemy types, three lives, achievements, a post-run upgrade shop, and Easy/Normal/Hardcore modes. Built with GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT/Codex and Claude.
